FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about government schemes

Who is eligible for these schemes?
Eligibility varies by scheme. You can find specific criteria on each scheme's detail page. Generally, schemes are designed for different groups including students, farmers, women, senior citizens, and economically weaker sections.
How can I apply for a scheme?
Each scheme has an "Apply Now" button that redirects you to the official government portal. You can also visit the respective department's office for offline applications.
Is there any fee for applying?
Most government schemes are free to apply. However, some may have a nominal processing fee. This information is clearly mentioned in the scheme details.
How long does it take to get approval?
Processing time varies by scheme and department. Typically, it can take anywhere from 30 to 90 days. You can track your application status on the official portal.
Are all schemes available in West Bengal?
We list both central and state schemes. Each scheme clearly mentions its coverage area - whether it's for West Bengal, all India, or specific states.
Can I get personalized scheme recommendations?
Yes! Create an account to receive personalized scheme recommendations based on your profile, location, and needs.
